## Introduction to AWS

- offers over 200 cloud services


**Benefits of AWS**

- the cloud has revolutionised business operations by offering scalable, on-demand resources.
- AWS gives the flexibility to scale up or down as needed. this flexibility and cost efficiency are the key reasons why millions of customers trust AWS to run their applications. 
- Key advantages:
	1. pay for what you use - AWS operates on a consumption-based model i.e., start small and pay for what has been used
		- this can lead to variable month-to-month costs
		- allows for opportunities to save
	2. economies of scale - as AWS expands its global capacity, it can offer lower pay-as-you-go costs due to its vast customer base
	3. no more guessing capacity - eliminates the guesswork of capacity planning. 
		- provision and scale resources to meet the exact needs
		- the ability to resize resources makes AWS elastic
	4. increased speed and agility - AWS enables rapid environment setup, allowing businesses to experiment with new ideas quickly and affordably
		- the lower cost of failure in the cloud encourages innovation and experimentation at a faster pace than on-premise solutions.
	5. save money on datacenters - AWS manages the infrastructure which allows businesses to focus on delivery value to their customers.
	6. go global in minutes - AWS's global reach allows businesses to quickly deploy applications in multiple regions with just a few clicks
