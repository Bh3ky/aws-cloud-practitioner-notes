## Introduction to the AWS Well-Architected Framework

- Well-Architected Framework provides cloud architects with strategies and guidelines for building resilient, scalable, and secure architectures on AWS.
- six pillars:
	1. **security** 
    
    - focuses on protecting data, systems, and assets. it ensures confidentiality through identity and access management & implements controls to detect and mitigate threats.

	2. **cost optimisation** 

    - avoid unnecessary expenses. making cost-effective decisions to ensure value on the cloud investment. 

	3. **performance efficiency** 
    
    - compute resources used effectively. optimal performance to meet demands. helps maintain efficiency as the workloads evolve.

	4. **sustainability**
    
    - minimise the environmental impact of running the cloud, aligning cloud strategy with broader environmental goals.

	5. **operational excellence** 
    
    - organise teams around business outcomes and implement observability for actionable insights. automate where possible. make frequent, small, reversible changes & refine operations procedures frequently. anticipate failure and learn from all operational events and metrics. use managed services


	6. **reliability**
    
    - automatically recover from failure. test recovery procedures. horizontal scaling. stop guessing capacity.
